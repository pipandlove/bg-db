# Data licence

The **data** of this project (the match records in `data/`, their metadata, attachments, and the catalogs and indexes derived from them)
is dedicated to the public domain under **CC0 1.0 Universal** (SPDX identifier `CC0-1.0`).

- Summary: <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>
- Full legal text: [LICENSE-DATA.txt](LICENSE-DATA.txt), a copy of <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode.txt>

The **code** (`packages/`, `scripts/`, `site/`) is licensed separately under the MIT licence: see [LICENSE](LICENSE).

## What this means in practice (a summary, not legal advice)

- Anyone may copy, use, change, redistribute and sell the data, with **no conditions**: no attribution and no obligation to share back.
  Crediting the project is appreciated, and each match records its `contributor` and source, but it is not required.
- A bare transcription of a match is a sequence of moves, which is largely factual. CC0 makes the position clear for whatever rights might exist
  anyway (in the compilation, the database, the notes people add) by waiving them as far as the law of each country allows.
- **Contributors keep nothing back:** by submitting a match you dedicate your contribution to the public domain, and you state that you have the right to do so.
  This cannot be undone for what has already been published.

## What is not covered

- Rights that a contributor cannot waive because they do not hold them: material from a tournament organiser or a site whose terms forbid redistribution,
  analysis output from software whose terms forbid it (spec RP-04), videos (the project hosts none, it only links to YouTube) and anything marked otherwise.
  Do not submit such material (spec RP-02, RP-06). If you believe something should not be here, open an issue: it will be removed from the published data (spec RP-03).
- Names and trademarks: the names of programs and sites mentioned (eXtreme Gammon, GNU Backgammon, YouTube, ...) belong to their owners.
